Level Up With Gaming Club

Gaming Club is a student gaming club for anyone who loves esports, casual co-op nights, and competitive tournaments. Join a squad, climb the leaderboard, and make friends who actually understand why you rage-quit last night.

Join The Club See Tournaments

Why Gamers Join Gaming Club

We are more than a Discord server. We run real events, coach new players, and give every member a place on a scoreboard that actually matters.

  • Weekly tournaments: Ranked brackets across multiple games with real prizes.
  • Squad matchmaking: We pair you with teammates who match your rank and playstyle.
  • Coaching sessions: Learn strategy from senior members who've won regional events.
  • LAN nights: Monthly offline meetups with big-screen matches and snacks.

How To Get Started

  1. Fill out the registration form and pick your main game.
  2. Get invited to our Discord and matched with a squad.
  3. Play your placement matches and enter the leaderboard.
