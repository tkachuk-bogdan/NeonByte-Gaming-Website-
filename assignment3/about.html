About Gaming Club

Gaming Club started in a university dorm room with two monitors, one router, and a shared love of competitive gaming. What began as a weekly LAN night between friends has grown into a full club with ranked scrims, coaching, and a real tournament calendar.

Our Mission

We want every student, from total beginner to aspiring pro, to have a squad, a coach, and a stage to compete on.

What We Value

  • Fair play - no smurfing, no toxicity, no exceptions
  • Growth - every player should leave better than they joined
  • Community - it's a squad first, a scoreboard second
